🏛️ About PSVPEC

Prince Shri Venkateshwara Padmavathy Engineering College (PSVPEC), situated in Ponmar, Chennai, is an autonomous institution established with the mission to impart quality engineering education, promote applied research, and nurture ethical professionals.

Equipped with state-of-the-art computational infrastructure, industry-supported Centers of Excellence, and dedicated faculty mentorship, the college consistently achieves outstanding academic and placement milestones.

⚡ CSE & IT Departments

The Departments of Computer Science & Engineering and Information Technology represent the epicenters of technological dynamism at PSVPEC.

From cloud architectures and artificial intelligence to cybersecurity and modern web engineering, the departments foster a vibrant ecosystem of hackathons, symposiums, technical paper forums, and open-source developer communities.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Who is eligible to participate in AuraFeast'26?
1st and 2nd year undergraduate students belonging to any recognized engineering, technology, arts, and science institutions are eligible to register and participate.
Can I participate in multiple events?
Yes! You can participate in multiple events as long as the event schedules do not clash. We recommend selecting 1 Technical and 1 or 2 Non-Technical events.
Will lunch and participation certificates be provided?
Yes. All registered attendees receive an official Certificate of Participation, a welcome kit, and a networking lunch feast.
What should I bring on the day of the event?
Please bring your valid College ID Card (mandatory for gate entry and verification) , a laptop for tech events and any event-specific materials (such as your presentation slides or camera).
How do I reach the college campus?
PSVPEC is conveniently located at Ponmar (near Medavakkam / Mambakkam), Chennai. College buses and public MTC transport routes are readily available from major city hubs including Tambaram, Medavakkam, and Koyambedu.
