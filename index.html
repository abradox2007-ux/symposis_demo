🏛️ About PSVPEC

Prince Shri Venkateshwara Padmavathy Engineering College (PSVPEC), situated in Ponmar, Chennai, is an autonomous institution established with the mission to impart quality engineering education, promote applied research, and nurture ethical professionals.

Equipped with state-of-the-art computational infrastructure, industry-supported Centers of Excellence, and dedicated faculty mentorship, the college consistently achieves outstanding academic and placement milestones.

⚡ CSE & IT Departments

The Departments of Computer Science & Engineering and Information Technology represent the epicenters of technological dynamism at PSVPEC.

From cloud architectures and artificial intelligence to cybersecurity and modern web engineering, the departments foster a vibrant ecosystem of hackathons, symposiums, technical paper forums, and open-source developer communities.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Who is eligible to participate in Aurafest'26?
All years undergraduate students (1st, 2nd, 3rd, and final year) belonging to any recognized engineering, technology, arts, and science institutions are eligible to register and participate.
What are the registration fees and Combo Offers?
We offer single and team combo packages:
  • Single Pass: ₹300 / person (Participate in all events you like)
  • 2 Persons Combo: ₹499 total (Save ₹101 — ₹249.50 per person, participate in all events you like)
  • 3 Persons Trio Combo: ₹749 total (Save ₹151 — ₹250 per person, participate in all events you like)
  • 4 Persons Team Combo: ₹999 total (Save ₹201 — Full squad pass, participate in all events you like)
All passes include lunch feast, welcome delegate kits, and certificates for every participant.
How many events can I participate in after paying the registration fee?
There is no restriction! When you pay the registration fee of ₹300/- (or register via any combo pass), each participant can participate in every event they like across all 11 technical and non-technical competitions.
Is lunch provided with the registration fee?
Yes, absolutely! A delicious complimentary networking lunch feast is fully included with every registration pass (both single ₹300 passes and combo offers) along with official certificates and welcome delegate kits.
What should I bring on the day of the event?
Please bring your valid College ID Card (mandatory for gate entry and verification), a laptop for tech events, and any event-specific materials (such as your presentation slides or camera).
How do I reach the college campus?
PSVPEC is conveniently located at Ponmar (near Medavakkam / Mambakkam), Chennai. Public MTC transport routes are readily available from major city hubs including Tambaram, Medavakkam, and Koyambedu.
