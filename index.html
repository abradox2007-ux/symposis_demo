About

Hosted by PSVPEC

AuraFeast'26 is organised by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering and the Department of Information Technology at Prince Shri Venkateshwara Padmavathy Engineering College, Ponmar, Chennai — bringing together students from across colleges for a day of building, competing, and connecting. Held on 15th September at the PSVPEC Mini Auditorium.
